For readers

A name, and a place the letter can land.

Use a PO box or a virtual mailbox. A street address works, and we treat it as highly sensitive: only the writer matched to you right now can see it, and only for that match.

At least 15 characters. Confirm the email before the address is stored. Authenticator-app MFA is offered once the account exists, and is a good idea if you store a mailing address.
