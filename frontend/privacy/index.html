Stranger Thoughts

Privacy

Draft, for review before any real personal information is collected in a deployed environment. This is not a finished legal policy.

What we ask for

A writer account needs an email and a pseudonym. Optional descriptors — age range (18 and older only), a coarse region, race, and an economic-status range — are stored only if you choose them. They are not used to pick a match, and you can delete them without deleting the account.

A reader account needs an email, a pseudonym, and a mailing address so a matched writer can send a letter. A PO box or virtual mailbox is the address we hope you use. A street address is accepted and treated as highly sensitive.

We do not collect information from anyone under 13, and the age question has no child ranges.

Email

The email you verify at sign-up is for the account and, unless you turn it off in account settings, for a short note when a stranger marks a letter mailed. That note does not include a name, an address, a city, or the letter. The email is never shown to the stranger and is not returned by the account settings API.

Who can see a mailing address

The address is encrypted before it is stored. It is shown only to the writer who is currently matched to that reader, and only for that match. It is not included in letter lists, older matches, or logs. When you rotate, the previous writer can no longer read it.

Keeping a copy

A private copy and an archive offer are off unless you turn them on. They are not how a letter is sent. Archive text stays pending until a person curates it. Preserved pages do not store a street address, an email, or a legal name. The public archive is a static page, not an open API.

Payments

Tips use Stripe Checkout and Stripe Connect. Card numbers are entered on Stripe, not on this site. Connect Express identity checks are Stripe’s. Until the owner turns on live payments, the service is built for Stripe test mode only.

Deletion

Deleting an account removes the profile, the optional descriptors, and the encrypted address. Letter rows that other people still need for their own history keep a match id and a timestamp, with the pseudonym replaced by “Removed”. Payment rows keep ids and amounts so a tip can be reconciled. Backups from point-in-time recovery can hold ciphertext for up to 35 days.

The full inventory is in the repository at docs/SECURITY-REVIEW.md.
