My letters

    How to send a letter

    • Plain paper.
    • A pen that will not bleed through.
    • A standard envelope and a stamp.
    • A PO box or virtual mailbox as the return address.
    • Never a home address.

    The address on this page is for the letter you are sending now. Older matches stay in the log as a name, a date, and whether you sent it.
