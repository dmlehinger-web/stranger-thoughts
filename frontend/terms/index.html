Stranger Thoughts

Terms

Draft, for review before a deployed environment collects real personal information.

Stranger Thoughts matches a writer with a reader at random among readers who are active. Optional descriptors are not an input to that choice. You may rotate to a new stranger a limited number of times per day. Past matches stay in your log as a name, a date, and a sent mark.

Letters are physical mail. The site tracks that a letter was marked mailed or received. It does not take a typed letter or a photo of one as the letter. Mailing address coming soon: no street address is shown until a non-residential desk mailbox is ready.

Your verified email can receive a quiet note when a stranger marks a letter mailed. You can turn that off in account settings. The note and the on-page status do not name the sender or their location. A desk pseudonym may still label the match.

Keeping a private copy, or offering words to the Stranger Archive, is optional and off unless you choose it. An archive offer is not published until a person curates it. Do not put an email, a street, or a legal name in that text.

You will not send a home address as a return address, and you will not use the service to harass anyone. A reader can report a writer, which blocks that writer from being matched to them again.

Tips are optional. A tip to a reader is meant for that reader. A separate tip, pay what you think this is worth, supports the people who keep the site running. The platform fee on reader tips is 0% unless a later notice says otherwise. Stripe handles payout identity checks. Test mode does not move real money.

You must be 18 or older to make an account. We do not offer the service to children.
