# Terms draft

This is a draft for review before a deployed environment collects real personal information. The same draft is on the v2 site at `frontend/terms/`.

Stranger Thoughts matches a writer with a reader at random among readers who are active. Optional descriptors are not an input to that choice. A writer may rotate to a new stranger a limited number of times per day. Past matches stay in the log as a name, a date, and a sent mark. The log does not keep a copy of the mailing address.

You will not use a home address as a return address. You will not use the service to harass anyone. A reader can report a writer, which blocks that writer from being matched to them again.

Tips are optional. A tip on a letter is for that reader, looked up from the match. A separate tip, pay what you think this is worth, supports the people who keep the site running. The platform fee on a reader tip is 0% unless a later notice says otherwise. Stripe handles payout identity checks. Test mode does not move real money.

You must be 18 or older to make an account. The service is not offered to children.
